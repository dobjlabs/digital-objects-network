# Welcome to Bitcraft

Bitcraft is a crafting game. Your goal is to scale production of various digital resources, such as `Wood`, `Stone`, and `Iron`, in order to accumulate as many `Bit`s as you can.

Every item in Bitcraft is a "Digital Object," a cryptographically-certified `.dobj` file that lives on your computer. You can send and receive Digital Objects to other players by sending or receiving `.dobj` files.

## How do I get started?

Left click on a `.dobj` file in the file explorer on the left side of the screen to view details about it. Right click on the file to see a list of available actions you can perform on it. You can start off by right clicking your `Pocketknife` file to gather some wood.

Some actions can only be performed on groups of files. For example, crafting a `WoodenAxe` requires three input objects: one `Wood` and two `Stick`s. You can select multiple objects at once with cmd-, ctrl-, or shift-click. Right clicking on the group selection will then show you the available actions that may be executed on the group.

You can learn more about the different kinds of items you can produce by clicking on the recipebook icon at the top bar of this client.

## What's my goal?

You are trying to produce as many `Bit`s as possible. `Bit`s are made from `Wood`, `Stone`, and `Iron`. You can increase your rate of production of these base resources by crafting tools like axes, quarries, and mining drills, or by more cleverly utilizing and orchestrating compute.

Good luck!
