# Welcome to Bitcraft

Bitcraft is a crafting game. Your goal is to scale production of various digital resources, such as `Wood`, `Stone`, and `Iron`, in order to accumulate as many `BIT`s as you can.

Every item in Bitcraft is a "Digital Object," a cryptographically-certified `.dobj` file that lives on your computer.

## How do I get started?

Left click on a `.dobj` file in the file explorer on the left side of the screen to view details about it.

Right click on the file to see a list of available actions you can perform on it. Try right clicking on your pocketknife.

Some actions can only be performed on groups of files. You can select multiple objects at once with `cmd-`, `ctrl-`, or `shift-`click. Right clicking on the group selection will then show you the available actions that may be executed with the group.

- For example, crafting a `WoodenAxe` requires three input objects: one `Wood` and two `Stick`s. 

## What's my goal?

You are trying to produce as many `BIT`s as possible. `BIT`s are made from `Wood`, `Stone`, or `Iron`. You can increase your rate of production of these base resources by crafting tools like axes, quarries, and mining drills.

Happy crafting!
